# Part 4

After a while, the adventurers try to change up their strategy of food distribution to improve their morale. Each day you will choose a food item for each adventurer. The satisfaction points per day must be 5. Given a liked food gives 2 satisfaction, neutral food is 1 satisfaction, disliked food is 0.5 satisfaction. If an adventurer eats disliked food, they cannot eat a disliked food again the next day. Given a String array of stock *s* which contain food items and their amounts, a 2D array of preferences *p*, return a valid 2D array of the food consumed each day.

## Contraints

- 1 <= *s.length* <= 12
- *p.length* = 12
